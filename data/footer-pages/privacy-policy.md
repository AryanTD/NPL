# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated**: August 2026

NPL Auction ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform ("Service").

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Registration & Account Data**:
- Email address
- Full name (display name)
- Password (hashed and encrypted)
- Profile picture (via Google OAuth)
- Authentication provider information

**Gameplay Data**:
- Auction history and bid records
- Squad composition and player selections
- Game statistics (wins, average bid price, etc.)
- Timestamps of all actions
- IP address and device information

**Communication**:
- Feedback, bug reports, or messages sent through support
- Contact form submissions

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Session Data**:
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Referral source
- Pages visited and time spent
- Cookies and local storage data

**Technical Data**:
- Server logs
- Socket.io connection events
- API response times and errors
- Network performance metrics

### 1.3 Guest Users

Guest users can play without creating an account. We collect:
- Session-specific gameplay data (bids, squad selections)
- Temporary device identifiers (stored in localStorage)
- Technical metrics (performance, errors)

Guest data is **not retained** after the session ends and is **not linked to any personal identity**.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

- **Service Delivery**: Running auctions, managing accounts, processing bids
- **Personalization**: Remembering your preferences, display name, game history
- **Analytics**: Understanding user behavior, improving gameplay
- **Security**: Detecting fraud, enforcing Terms & Conditions
- **Communication**: Sending account updates, bug fixes, new features (opt-in)
- **Leaderboards**: Displaying your stats and rankings (if signed in)
- **Research**: Improving AI bot behavior and game balance (anonymized data only)

## 3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

### 3.1 Third-Party Services

We share data with the following services:

**Authentication**:
- **Google OAuth**: Handles sign-in, we receive your name and profile picture
- **Resend**: Manages email-based magic link authentication

**Database & Hosting**:
- **Neon PostgreSQL**: Hosts our database (ISO 27001 certified)
- **Render**: Hosts our server (SOC 2 compliant)
- **Vercel**: Hosts our frontend (enterprise-grade security)

**Analytics** (optional):
- We may use anonymized analytics tools to track platform usage (no personal data sent)

### 3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law:
- Court orders or subpoenas
- Government requests
- Protection of safety or legal rights

### 3.3 Business Transfers

If NPL Auction is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change and any choices you may have regarding your information.

### 3.4 No Third-Party Marketing

**We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.**

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption**: Data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (PostgreSQL encryption)
- **Authentication**: Secure password hashing (bcrypt), OAuth 2.0
- **Access Control**: Role-based access, limited employee access
- **Regular Audits**: Security reviews and penetration testing
- **Incident Response**: Procedures in place for data breach notification

**However**, no security system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

## 5. Data Retention

- **Signed-in Users**: Account data retained until you delete your account
- **Guest Users**: Session data deleted immediately after logout
- **Audit Logs**: Server logs retained for 90 days for security purposes
- **Deleted Accounts**: Personal data deleted within 30 days; gameplay statistics may be retained in anonymized form for analytics

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Update or correct inaccurate information
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and data (right to be forgotten)
- **Portability**: Request your data in a portable format
- **Opt-Out**: Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at **privacy@nplaution.com**.

## 7. Cookies & Local Storage

### 7.1 Cookies

We use cookies for:
- **Session Management**: Keeping you logged in
- **Authentication**: Storing session tokens
- **Preferences**: Remembering your tour preferences (if dismissed)

### 7.2 Local Storage

We store in browser local storage:
- **Guest ID**: Temporary identifier for guest sessions
- **Guest Name**: Display name for guest players
- **Tour Flags**: Whether you've seen onboarding tutorials

You can clear local storage at any time through browser settings. This will clear your guest identity and preferences.

### 7.3 Third-Party Cookies

Google OAuth may set cookies for authentication. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

## 8. Children's Privacy

NPL Auction is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information promptly.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Our servers are hosted in the United States (Render) and Europe (Neon). By using NPL Auction, you consent to the transfer of your information outside your country of residence.

## 10. Updates to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes via email or by posting the updated policy on our website. Your continued use following such notifications constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
